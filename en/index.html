A day in the shop, from the first box to the last receipt.

What slows you down today, and what Joli Comptoir changes. Pick a moment of the day.

Forty items to label before opening

Today
The box arrives, you write the prices by hand, opening time slips and a wrong price always gets through in the end.
With Joli Comptoir
Scan or search for the products and the till prints price and barcode in your label format. A whole category in one click.

Result: the door opens on time, and the price on the rail matches the till.

Six people in the queue, a price to track down

Today
You hunt for the price, key it in by hand, the queue grows. At the back of the shop, a customer puts the item down and walks out.
With Joli Comptoir
One beep per item, card or cash, receipt printed. A sale is wrapped up in seconds, and stock updates itself.

Result: every customer leaves served, even at the height of Saturday.

The jumper is too big, she's back to exchange it

Today
Finding the sale, correcting the till, keeping a record for the accounts: it all gets patched together in the corner of a notebook.
With Joli Comptoir
You find the receipt and pick the returned line. The credit note is signed and linked to the original sale, and the item goes back into stock.

Result: an exchange settled with a smile, and a customer who comes back.

Ten hours on your feet, and still the accounts

Today
Recounting, copying out totals, preparing figures for the accountant. The selling day stretches into an evening of data entry.
With Joli Comptoir
The daily closing is already calculated: receipts, VAT by rate, totals by payment method. At month end, the signed archive is ready for your accountant.

Result: you switch off the lights at 7.05pm.

    Everything you need to sell, close at hand.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Scan and sell
    Scanner, card or cash, receipt printed. Prices come from the catalogue and are always up to date.
    
        
    Label your shelves
    Price and EAN-13 barcode in your label format, on a Zebra printer or your office printer.
    
        
    Know your stock
    Every sale updates it. Stocktaking is done with the scanner, rail by rail, one beep per item.
    
        
    Handle returns and exchanges
    A signed credit note, linked to the exact line on the original receipt, and the item back in stock.
    
        
    Sell beyond the catalogue
    Open pricing for custom gift boxes, a miscellaneous item for an alteration or gift wrapping.
    
        
    Bring over your catalogue
    Products, photos and prices imported straight from your Shopify store.
    
        
    Stay compliant
    Tamper-proof journal, daily, monthly and annual closings, signed archives and tax export.
    
        
    Sell whatever happens
    The till and its data live in the shop: it keeps taking payments when the internet connection drops.
