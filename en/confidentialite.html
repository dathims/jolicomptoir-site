Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Translation provided for information only. Only the French version is legally binding. Read the French version.

In short: this website sets no cookies and does not track your visits. The Joli Comptoir software keeps the shop's sales on the shop's own computer, and it records neither the names nor the contact details of the shop's customers.

Data controller

OXYGENE NUMERIQUE (Oxynum), a simplified joint-stock company (SAS) with share capital of €10,000, 111 avenue Victor Hugo, 75016 Paris, RCS Paris 749 947 297, represented by Mr Rudy THIMOTHEE, President. Contact: bonjour@jolicomptoir.com, +33 6 61 02 75 26.

When you visit this website

  • No cookies, no trackers, no audience measurement or advertising tools.
  • No third-party services are called by the pages: fonts and images are served from the website itself.
  • The till demonstration runs in your browser. Nothing you do in it is transmitted or stored.

Like any host, GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) receives the technical data needed to display the pages, including your IP address, and processes it to ensure the security and proper operation of the service. As GitHub is established in the United States, this data may be processed there. The terms of this processing and the safeguards applied to transfers are described in GitHub's privacy statement. OXYGENE NUMERIQUE has no access to these logs.

When you write to us

If you contact us by email or telephone, we use the information you give us (name, contact details, shop, content of the message) to reply to you, prepare a quotation and follow up on your request commercially.

  • Legal basis: pre-contractual measures taken at your request, then our legitimate interest in following up the business relationship.
  • Recipients: only the OXYGENE NUMERIQUE staff handling your request, and our email provider as a technical service provider. Your data is never sold or rented.
  • Retention period: three years after our last exchange if no contract is concluded. If you become a client, for the duration of the contract, then in line with the applicable accounting and tax retention periods.

When you use the Joli Comptoir software

The software is installed on the shop's computer. The sales journal, closings, archives, stock and settings are stored locally on it. OXYGENE NUMERIQUE has no access to them and receives no sales data.

  • The software records neither the names, nor the contact details, nor the bank details of the shop's customers. Card payments are processed by the shop's card terminal, which is independent of the software.
  • The key that signs the journal is kept in the secure keychain of the computer's operating system.
  • Importing the catalogue from Shopify reads the public information on the shop's products (titles, prices, photos).

The shop remains responsible for the data it processes with the software. If it requests support that requires access to its computer or its data, OXYGENE NUMERIQUE acts as a processor, on the shop's instructions and only for the duration of the intervention, in accordance with Article 28 of the GDPR and the general terms and conditions.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, as well as the right to object to processing based on our legitimate interest, in particular for marketing purposes. You may also set out instructions regarding what happens to your data after your death.

To exercise these rights, write to bonjour@jolicomptoir.com or to OXYGENE NUMERIQUE, 111 avenue Victor Hugo, 75016 Paris, France. We reply within one month.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (the French data protection authority), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France, cnil.fr.

Changes

This policy may change, for example if the website adds a form or audience measurement. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
